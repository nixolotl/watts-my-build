About

What is WattsMyBuild?

WattsMyBuild is a PC build planner. You tell us how much you can spend and what you will use the PC for, and we suggest complete part lists that fit your budget.

How the builds are chosen

  • We start from the cheapest complete PC and upgrade the part that needs the money most.
  • The use case changes the priorities. Gaming puts more money into the graphics card. Work and study put more into the CPU, memory and storage. Video editing splits the money between CPU and GPU.
  • You get three options: Value (about 80% of your budget), Balanced (about 92%) and Max (all of it).
  • The power supply is picked automatically, so it always has enough power for your CPU and graphics card plus some spare headroom.
  • A stronger CPU can force a better cooler. We do that for you, and tell you when it happens.
  • Not happy with a part? Use Swap to change it and see how the total changes.
