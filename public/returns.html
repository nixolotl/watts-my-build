Returns

This is a sample policy for a school project demo. WattsMyBudget is not a real store, so nothing is actually sold or shipped.

Items that arrive damaged or not working

Report the problem within 7 days of delivery. Send your order number, a description of the problem, and photos or the unboxing video. If the part is confirmed defective, we replace it.

Changing your mind

If the order has not been handed to the courier yet, contact us and we can cancel it. After that, returns for a change of mind are not accepted, because PC parts are made to order from your build.

Warranty

Every part is covered by its manufacturer's warranty. Keep your order confirmation. The warranty period depends on the part and the brand, and the manufacturer decides whether a claim is accepted.

What is not covered

  • Damage caused by dropping, liquids, overheating or wrong installation.
  • Parts that have been opened, modified or had their seals or stickers removed.
  • Software problems, viruses and normal wear.

Refunds

If a replacement is not possible, we refund the price of the affected part to the payment method you used.

Start a return from the Contact page.
