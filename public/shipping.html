Shipping

Delivery area

We deliver across the Philippines by courier.

Processing and delivery time

  • Processing: 1 to 3 business days. Each PC is checked before it is packed.
  • Metro Manila: about 2 to 4 business days after processing.
  • Other areas: about 4 to 8 business days after processing. Remote islands can take longer.

Shipping cost

Shipping is free for every order.

Payment on delivery

You can choose cash on delivery at checkout. Please have the exact amount ready for the courier.

When your order arrives

  • Check the box for damage before you sign for it.
  • We recommend recording a short video as you open it. It helps if you need to report a problem.
  • If the courier cannot reach you, they will try again. After repeated failed attempts the order is returned to us.

Wrong address?

Contact us as soon as possible, before the order is handed to the courier. Use the details on the Contact page and include your order number.
